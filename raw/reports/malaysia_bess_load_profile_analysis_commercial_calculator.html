Regulatory Period 4 (RP4) Tariff Restructuring & Peak Demand Economics

On 1 July 2025, Tenaga Nasional Berhad (TNB) and Suruhanjaya Tenaga introduced the RP4 tariff framework (2025–2027), replacing legacy commercial/industrial tariffs (C1, C2, E1, E2, E3) with a standardized Medium Voltage (MV) schedule. This reform drastically shifted cost recovery from volumetric energy charges (sen/kWh) to fixed capacity and network demand charges (RM/kW), creating a high-stakes environment for facilities with spiky load profiles.

Interactive Tariff & Post-Solar MD Distortion Simulator

The Post-Solar Load Factor Distortion Effect: When a factory installs rooftop solar PV without storage, it reduces volumetric kWh consumption during sunny hours. However, because a single overcast 30-minute transient or late afternoon run (17:00–22:00) sets the full monthly Maximum Demand, the MD share of the electricity bill surges from ~55% up to ~70%. Peninsular Malaysia Geographic Density & Target Industrial Parks

Peninsular Malaysia features over 500 industrial parks and 22 Free Industrial Zones (FIZs). Target industries are sharply segregated by state: Selangor (Klang Valley) and Johor (Iskandar) concentrate the densest clusters of Tier 1 spiky-load target plants, whereas Penang concentrates flat 24/7 semiconductor fabs.

Go-To-Market Ecosystem & Decision-Maker Engagement

BESS origination requires engaging two key personas: Technical Specifiers (Registered Electrical Energy Managers - REEM, Certified Energy Managers, Facility Managers) and Financial Approvers (CFO, Managing Director). Reaching these stakeholders is catalyzed by Malaysia's Energy Efficiency and Conservation Act (EECA) and ISO 50001 mandatory requirements.

FMM (Federation of Malaysian Mfrs)

3,500+ Member Factories

Malaysia's primary industrial body with active state committees in Selangor, Penang, and Johor.

Key Touchpoints & Events: FMM Energy Efficiency & Conservation Conference; FMM ISO 50001 EnMS Workshops; State Branch ESG Seminars.

MPMA (Plastics Mfrs Assoc)

800+ Plastics & Injection Plants

Direct access to factory owners hit with 40%+ MD bill shares from heater bands and chillers.

Key Touchpoints & Events: MPMA Technical Seminars on RP4 Tariff Mitigation; Mould & Die Pavilion at MIMF (MITEC KL).

MAREMA & MAESCO

ST-Certified REEMs & ESCOs

The professional guild of registered energy managers auditing 15-min load profiles.
