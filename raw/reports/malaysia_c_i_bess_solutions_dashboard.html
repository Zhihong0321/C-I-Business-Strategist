RP4 Tariff Architecture & Maximum Demand Catalyst

Under Regulatory Period 4 (RP4, governing Peninsular Malaysia through 31 December 2027), Tenaga Nasional Berhad (TNB) and the Energy Commission (ST) unbundled legacy tariffs into Low Voltage (LV), Medium Voltage (MV), and High Voltage (HV) tiers. Maximum Demand (MD) charges are split into an explicit Capacity Charge (generation reserve) and Network Charge (grid stress). A single 30-minute peak draw recorded by smart meters during the billing cycle sets the baseline charge for the entire month.

⚡ The MV ToU Tariff Trap (Unmanaged Migration)

Migrating from MV General to MV ToU raises maximum demand rates by +8.7% (from RM89.27 to RM97.06/kW) and increases peak volumetric energy rates to 31.32 sen/kWh. Without active demand control or energy storage, an uncoordinated peak spike during weekday afternoons results in severe financial penalties.

🔋 The BESS + ToU Optimization Opportunity

Under MV ToU, TNB calculates Capacity and Network charges exclusively on peak demand recorded between 2:00 PM and 10:00 PM on weekdays. Maximum demand spikes occurring off-peak (10:00 PM – 2:00 PM and weekends) are completely exempt. BESS discharges dynamically during peak hours to cap grid draw.

Solar & Tariff Mismatch Notice: Peak solar irradiance occurs from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM (Off-Peak tariff). TNB's expensive Peak Tariff starts at 2:00 PM and extends to 10:00 PM. BESS bridges this gap by charging midday and discharging from 2:00 PM to 10:00 PM to keep net grid draw below the dynamic MD target.
1. Midday Charging (10am–1pm)

BESS absorbs excess solar PV generation or off-peak grid energy to prevent zero-export clipping under SELCO guidelines.

2. Peak Discharge (2pm–10pm)

AI-driven EMS releases stored energy when factory equipment startups risk breaching the pre-defined target threshold.

3. Capped Grid Demand

TNB smart meter records the reduced net grid peak, locking in low Capacity and Network charges for the billing cycle.

Regulatory, BOMBA Safety & GITA Tax Framework

Navigating mandatory Malaysian grid licensing, BOMBA MS 2747:2024 fire safety certification, and Budget 2027 tax allowance rules.

Suruhanjaya Tenaga (ST)

Licensing & Safety Mandate

  • Threshold: Mandatory licensing for BESS ≥ 5 kW under Electricity Supply Act 1990.
  • Guidelines GP/ST/No.62/2026: Mandatory IEC 62619, IEC 63056, UL 9540A certification.
  • Certification: Form G (Completion) & Form H (Testing) signed by ST Competent Person.
BOMBA Fire Safety

MS 2747:2024 Standards

  • Fire Rating: 2-hour fire-rated enclosure or separating wall required for stationary BESS.
  • Safety Clearance: Mandatory thermal runaway suppression and isolation clearances.
  • Insurance Impact: Fire Certificate (FC) is required to maintain valid industrial insurance.
Grid Interconnection

TNB & GSO Regulations

  • CAS Study: Connection Assessment Study required for SELCO installations > 72 kWp.
  • Mandatory BESS: Required for all new SELCO systems exceeding 1 MWac capacity.
  • CRESS Package: Grid access charges set at 14 sen/kWh (developers) / 4 sen/kWh (consumers).
