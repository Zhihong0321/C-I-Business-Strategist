## Why the repo can't answer it
Only one source, unverified, covers GTFS 5.0: R6 (`kb/sources/R6_financing_2026.md` §2). It gives a 0% interest rebate, a 60%–80% guarantee and RM1.0B to 31 Dec 2026. Its exclusions are narrow: the "User" category excludes *standalone* solar, and the "Producer" category excludes LSS and rooftop solar PV. Integrated solar + BESS is not excluded. R6's own recommender still finances solar through GTFS 5.0 (extraction note 7; `kb/registers/internal_issues.md`). R4 and R8 name GTFS 5.0 as a solar financing route but do not explain it. No file mentions MGTC proposing GTFS 6.0, or what happens to GTFS after 31 Dec 2026.
